Professional Summary & Philosophy

I am a Software Engineer with deep expertise in Native Android Development (Kotlin & Java) and enterprise backend architecture powered by PHP / Laravel RESTful APIs.

At Universitas Semarang (USM), I architect and maintain mission-critical applications including USM SIMA Mobile App (Academic Information Management for students and faculty), USM Attendance & Lecturer Performance App (GPS Geofencing location tracking), USM Executive Analytics Dashboard (Executive Information System for university leadership), and the Tracer Study Backend API System.

Additionally, I have successfully delivered field workforce systems for public sector entities, including location-based attendance tracking for the Public Works Department (DPU) and regional SME showcase mobile apps.
